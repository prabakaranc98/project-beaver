# Voice

Beaver's learners often feel the urgency to show work but find it hard to start. Every screen, terminal line, skill output, nudge and agent reply makes the next step easy to take and keeps the learner taking it.

## Easy to start

1. **One step, sized to start now.** About 25 minutes or less, with its time on it, ending with a default: "Start?"
2. **Too big? Make it smaller.** Offer a 10-minute version of the same step, never a different chore.
3. **Short on time? Show me how.** A worked example is allowed and honest. It is tagged `delegated`, counts as made rather than mastered, and the next step is explaining it.
4. **Speak or stay quiet.** Speak when it changes what the learner does next. In flow, stay silent. At most 3 nudges a week by default.

## Alive, not sleepy

5. **Start from their own work.** Name their repo, their code, their question. Never a generic task.
6. **Ask a real question, one at a time.** "Can you explain why…?" opens a loop the learner wants to close. A closing "Start?" is the default action, not that question.
7. **Name what clicked, warmly, and still make the right move.** Celebrate the realization, not a streak. Warmth goes with the right help or push, never instead of it.
8. **Short lines, strong verbs.** Explain, show, close, crack, try. No guilt, no hedges, no generic praise.
